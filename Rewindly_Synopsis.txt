Project Synopsis: Rewindly
Date: July 4, 2025

Overview:
Rewindly is a modern web and mobile application designed to recreate the nostalgic experience of using cassette players and tapes. The platform allows users to play, manage, and customize music playlists with retro-inspired visuals and interactive cassette animations. Rewindly integrates with popular music services like Spotify and Shazam, providing seamless music search, playback, and personalized features.

Key Features:
- Cassette player and tape animations synchronized with music playback
- Playlist creation, editing, and management
- Theme customization with a color picker and multiple cassette styles
- Integration with Spotify and Shazam APIs for music search and playback
- Audio visualizer for real-time music visualization
- Responsive design for both desktop and mobile devices

Technology Stack:
- Frontend: React, Vite, Tailwind CSS
- Mobile: Capacitor (Android support)
- APIs: Spotify, Shazam
- State Management: React hooks, local storage

Target Users:
- Music enthusiasts seeking a unique, retro-themed listening experience
- Users who enjoy customizing their music player interface
- Individuals looking for seamless integration with modern music services

Vision:
Rewindly aims to blend the charm of vintage cassette players with the convenience of modern music streaming, offering users a fun, interactive, and highly customizable music experience.

Future Plans:
- User accounts and cloud-based playlist sync
- Premium features and exclusive cassette themes
- Support for additional music streaming services
